Dailyvokab

Learn the Thai you need for daily life.

Flashcards, audio and a quick quiz for over 2,000 Thai words and phrases. For iPhone.

Coming soon to the App Store as “Thai Flashcards: Dailyvokab”

What you get

  • Basics: 12 units in order. Start here.
  • Words: 80 units in 15 categories, such as Food & Drink and Getting Around.
  • Phrases: 16 units, such as Small Talk and Ordering Food & Drinks.
  • Audio for every word and phrase, in Thai and in English. The audio is an AI voice.
  • See the tones. Each flashcard draws the tone of every syllable.
  • Three ways to learn each unit: Flashcards, Audio (press play, then put your phone in your pocket) and a Test Quiz.
  • Smart review brings back the words you are still learning.
  • Daily word reminders and the Word of the hour widget put a Thai word on your Lock Screen.
  • No internet needed to learn. Every word and its audio is inside the app.

What is free

Dailyvokab is free to download. The first unit in each of Basics, Words and Phrases is free: three units in all.

Dailyvokab Pro opens every unit, and lets you make your own custom units. Pro is a weekly or yearly subscription that renews automatically. The app shows the price before you buy.
