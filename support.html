Dailyvokab Support

Help with Dailyvokab, the iPhone app for learning Thai words and phrases.

Contact us. Email williger09@gmail.com. In the app, you can also tap Settings → Contact us.

Please tell us your iPhone model and the app version. The version is in Settings → About → Version.

What is free

Dailyvokab is free to download. These three units are free:

  • Basics: 1. First words
  • Words: Politeness Essentials
  • Phrases: Greetings & Goodbyes

Smart review, the Daily word reminders and the Word of the hour widget work with the words from these units.

Everything else needs Dailyvokab Pro: all the other units, and your own custom units (also words you import from a file). Dailyvokab Pro is a subscription. You pay weekly or yearly, and it renews automatically until you cancel. The app shows the price for your country before you buy.

Restore Dailyvokab Pro

Did you buy Pro, and now the units are locked? This can happen on a new iPhone, or after you delete the app and install it again.

  1. Open Dailyvokab and tap Settings.
  2. Under Dailyvokab Pro, tap Restore purchases.
  3. When Pro is found, you see “Welcome back”.

You need internet for this. Use the same Apple Account that you bought Pro with. The Pro screen has Restore purchases too.

Do you see “No purchases found”? Check which Apple Account your iPhone uses for purchases: open the iPhone Settings app, tap your name, then tap Media & Purchases, then View Account. Sign in with the account you bought Pro with, then try again.

Do you see “Pro could not be checked”? Check your iPhone's date and time in the iPhone Settings app, under General → Date & Time. Then tap Restore purchases again.

Cancel your subscription

Apple handles all payments, so you cancel with Apple:

  1. Open the iPhone Settings app and tap your name.
  2. Tap Subscriptions.
  3. Tap Dailyvokab.
  4. Tap Cancel Subscription.

Or open this link on your iPhone: apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions. While Pro is active, the app has a shortcut too: Settings → Dailyvokab Pro → Manage subscription.

To stop the next payment, cancel at least 24 hours before it renews. After you cancel, Pro stays on until the end of the time you paid for. Under Settings → Dailyvokab Pro, the app then shows “Ends on” and the date. Deleting the app does not cancel the subscription.

Apple's own guide: Cancel a subscription from Apple.

Refunds

Apple handles all payments, so refunds come from Apple. We cannot see your payment, and we cannot give money back ourselves. To ask Apple for a refund:

  1. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com and sign in with your Apple Account.
  2. Choose I'd like to, then Request a refund.
  3. Choose the reason, then Next.
  4. Choose Dailyvokab Pro, then Submit.

Apple decides about each refund. Apple's own guide: Request a refund for apps or content that you bought from Apple.

Back up and restore your progress

Your progress is saved on your iPhone. If you back up your iPhone to iCloud or to a computer, your progress is in that backup too. You can also save your progress in a file yourself, for example before you change phones.

Make a backup

  1. In Dailyvokab, tap Settings.
  2. Under Your progress, tap Back up my progress.
  3. Choose where to keep the file. For example, tap Save to Files, then choose iCloud Drive.

The file name starts with “Dailyvokab-backup”. It holds your progress and your custom units. Your settings are not in it.

Restore from a backup

  1. In Dailyvokab, tap Settings.
  2. Under Your progress, tap Restore from a backup.
  3. Choose your backup file. The app shows what is in the backup and what is on this phone now.
  4. Tap Restore.

The backup replaces the progress, schedule, streak, goal, hidden words and custom units on this phone. Right after, you can tap Undo to get the old progress back. A backup does not bring back Dailyvokab Pro: for that, use Restore purchases (see above).

Questions

Is the voice a real person?

No. All the Thai and English audio uses an AI voice. It is not a recording of a real person. The app says this too, in Settings → About → Voice. Words you import from a file are read by your iPhone's own voice.

Does it work without internet?

Yes, for learning. All the words and their audio are inside the app. You need internet to buy Pro or to restore it. Pro also needs the internet sometimes, so the app can check that your plan is still active.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-in. Your progress is saved on your phone.

Is there a free trial?

No. Instead, three units are free, with no time limit (see “What is free”), so you can try the app before you buy.

Which iPhones does it work on?

Any iPhone with iOS 17 or later.

How do I stop the reminders?

In Dailyvokab, tap Settings. Under Reminders, open Daily word reminders or Smart review reminder and turn it off. To stop the Word of the hour widget, remove it from your Lock Screen or Home Screen.

A word looks wrong. What can I do?

Open the word and tap Report a problem with this word. Your email app opens, with the word already filled in. Tell us what is wrong, for example the sound, the meaning or the tones.

How do I get my data deleted?

See “Having your data deleted” in our privacy policy.
