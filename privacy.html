Thai Vocab Privacy Policy

Last updated 18 September 2026

Thai Vocab is a vocabulary app for people learning Thai. This page explains, in plain words, what the app collects and what it does not.

The short version. Your learning progress stays on your phone. The app sends anonymous usage statistics — which units and words people study, what they search for, where they stop — so we can improve it. Nothing that identifies you is ever sent, and you can switch this off in Settings.

What stays on your phone

Everything about your learning is stored only on your device: the words you have rated, your review schedule, streak, learning goal, custom units, reminder settings, and the name and answers you gave during onboarding. We have no account system and no server that holds this data. Deleting the app deletes it.

Word lists you import (for example a CSV from your teacher) are read on your phone and never uploaded.

Anonymous usage statistics

To understand how the app is used, it sends anonymous events to PostHog, an analytics service, hosted in the European Union. Examples of events:

  • a unit was opened, a flashcard session was finished, a quiz was completed with a score
  • a word was rated “Know it” or “Don’t know”, or hidden from practice
  • a search was made, and whether it found anything — the words people look for and cannot find tell us which words to add
  • which onboarding step was reached, and whether a learning goal was set
  • the screen being viewed, the app version, the phone model and system version, and the approximate country from the network address

Each installation gets a random identifier so that events from the same phone can be counted together. This identifier is not connected to your name, email, phone number, contacts or any account, and we do not attempt to work out who you are. We do not use this data to track you across other apps or websites, and we do not sell it or share it for advertising.

If the app crashes or hits an unexpected error, a technical report of the error is sent the same way, so we can fix it.

Switching it off

Open the app, go to Settings → App, and turn off Help improve the app. From that moment nothing is sent. Events queued while the phone was offline are discarded.

Notifications

Daily word reminders are scheduled on your phone by the app itself. They do not involve any server, and you control them in Settings.

Audio

All pronunciation audio is included in the app. Playing a word does not contact any server.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If the app starts collecting something new, this page will say so and the date at the top will change.
